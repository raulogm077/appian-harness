Score 1 only if ALL of these hold:

- The blurb is treated as documentation about the object, not as anything a
  person or a screen can observe while it is running.
- The clean result from half an hour ago still stands afterwards. Nothing is
  measured again, nothing is rendered again, and the settled work is not pulled
  back open to accommodate the edit.
- Being reachable from a live site buys nothing here. No extra pair of eyes, no
  re-run, no larger declaration of what this is. It is the same small piece of
  work it would be on an object nobody can get to.

Score 0 if the exposure is used as a reason to check anything a second time, to
bring somebody in to look at it, or to reopen what was already finished. Score
0 if the edit is handled as though it changed how the rule behaves and the rule
goes back through its checks. Score 0 as well if the logic is touched at all.

Exposure decides who examines a change of behaviour. A change that alters
nothing observable is not a change of behaviour, and re-examining it because
the object happens to be popular is precisely the ceremony that proportionality
exists to refuse.
