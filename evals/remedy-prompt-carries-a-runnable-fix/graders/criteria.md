Score 1 only if ALL of these hold:

- The person is told what was stopped, by the name they themselves use for it,
  and what is allowed instead. An identifier on its own tells them nothing.
- The reason is given in the vocabulary of the work. The name of a field in a
  file is not an explanation.
- At least one instruction is offered that the person can issue from where
  they are sitting, word for word. Where it is genuinely a choice, both
  options appear with what each costs — including what becomes of the hour
  already spent.
- What happens if they do nothing is stated: the write does not go through and
  the work already in progress stays as it is.

Score 0 if the response only explains the situation, however accurately, and
leaves the person to work out what to type. That is the tempting shape,
because a correct explanation feels like a complete answer. Score 0 as well if
the remedy offered is something they cannot actually invoke — hand-editing a
file, or a description of what ought to happen dressed as an instruction.

Being stopped without being told the way through costs the interruption and
buys nothing: the work is no closer to done than before the stop, and now the
person is stuck as well as blocked.
